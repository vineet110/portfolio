# Contact

## How to reach the studio
The contact section of the site has a form (name, email, company optional,
project type, and a message) that's saved and reviewed by the studio for
follow-up.

## Direct WhatsApp
For a faster first conversation, you can message the studio directly on
WhatsApp using the "Let's Connect" / "Chat with us on WhatsApp" link, found
in the contact section, the navigation, and the footer.

## What happens after you submit
Enquiries are saved and reviewed, and the studio follows up with next
steps — there's no automated instant reply beyond the on-site confirmation
that the enquiry was received.

## How to start a project
Use the contact form with your name, email, project type, and a short
message about what you're building, or message the studio directly on
WhatsApp. You can bring just an idea — you don't need a finished spec.
