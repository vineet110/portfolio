# About Sapvy

## Who we are
We're Sapvy, a creative digital studio that designs and builds websites and apps
for startups, businesses, and founders. We make websites and apps — if you
have an idea, we design and build it.

The studio's name is Sapvy. The website lives at sapvy.vercel.app.

## Who we work with
Mostly startups, small and medium businesses, and individual founders —
anyone who needs a digital product, most often a website or an app.

## How we work
A dedicated team takes your idea from concept to launch: idea, UI/UX,
design, development, and launch, handled start to finish rather than
handed off between separate vendors at each stage.

## What we don't claim
We don't publish client lists, revenue figures, team size, statistics,
testimonials, or awards, because none of that exists yet in a form we're
willing to put in front of people. The portfolio on this site is made up of
studio-built concept and experimental projects, not real client
engagements, and every one of them is clearly labeled as such.
