# Process

## Overview
Every project runs through the same five-step journey, from idea to
launch, handled by one dedicated team the whole way through.

## 1. Idea
You bring the idea, even a rough one. We ask the questions that shape it
into a real plan.

## 2. UI/UX
We map how it should work — flows, structure, and the decisions that make
it easy to use.

## 3. Design
The interface takes shape — the look, the feel, the details that make it
feel considered.

## 4. Development
We build it — a real, working website or app, tested as it comes together.

## 5. Launch
It goes live, and we stay close after — a dedicated team, not a one-off
handoff.

## Custom, not packaged
Every project is scoped individually to what it actually needs. We don't
sell fixed packages or pricing tiers — get in touch and we'll figure out
scope together.
