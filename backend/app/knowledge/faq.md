# Frequently asked questions

## What do you do?
We make websites and apps. If you have an idea, we design and build it —
from idea through UI/UX, design, development, and launch.

## What services do you offer?
Exactly three: Websites (including web applications and AI-integrated
experiences), Mobile Apps, and UI/UX Design. Nothing else is part of the
current offering.

## Do you build web apps?
Yes — web applications are part of the Websites service, from a landing
page to a full custom web application.

## Do you build AI websites?
Yes, when AI genuinely helps the product we build it in as part of a
website or web app. AI isn't a separate service on its own.

## Do you build mobile apps?
Yes — custom mobile app design and development for iOS and Android is one
of our three services.

## Do you do UI/UX design?
Yes — UI/UX design is one of our three services, and it's also the first
design step in every project we build.

## Who do you work with?
Mostly startups, small and medium businesses, and individual founders —
anyone who needs a website or an app built.

## Do you offer fixed packages?
No. Every project is scoped individually — get in touch through the
contact form or WhatsApp and we'll figure out scope together.

## Can I bring just an idea?
Yes. You don't need a finished spec or design — a rough idea is enough to
start the conversation, and the process (idea → UI/UX → design →
development → launch) takes it from there.

## What is your process?
Idea, UI/UX, design, development, launch — five steps, one dedicated team,
start to finish.

## Do you have a team?
Yes — a dedicated team takes your idea from concept to launch.

## Can I see your work?
Yes — the Work section on the site. These are studio-built concept and
experimental projects, clearly labeled CONCEPT or EXPERIMENTAL.

## Are these real client projects?
No. The current portfolio is studio-built concept and experimental work,
not real client engagements. Real client work will be added and clearly
marked as such once it exists.

## How can I contact you?
Use the contact form on the site, or reach out directly on WhatsApp for a
faster first conversation.

## Can I reach you on WhatsApp?
Yes — there's a direct WhatsApp link in the contact section, the nav, and
the footer.

## Do you list pricing on the site?
No. Pricing depends on scope, so it isn't published — get in touch and
we'll scope it together.
